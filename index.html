An idea in circulation

LOVE.

If people can believe in money, they can believe in love. LOVE is a token, an idea, and an invitation to participate in something whose name says what it stands for.

Why LOVE?

We are born into systems we did not choose. We inherit currencies, institutions, habits, and rules. We learn to use the things already around us, but that does not mean we have to spend our lives running away from them.

We can make things of our own. We can choose what we give attention to, what we share, and what we place our faith in.

My weapon is LOVE.

LOVE does not ask anyone to abandon dollars, agree with a doctrine, or promise allegiance. It simply puts another idea into circulation: that love is worth participating in.

What is the token?

LOVE (LOVE) is an Ethereum token associated with the Crousia project. People may acquire, hold, transfer, or trade it as they choose. Its market price is determined by market activity and can rise or fall.

There is no promise of profit, guaranteed value, redemption, or particular future use. The token is not a charitable donation, and acquiring it does not create a tax-deductible contribution.

A note on money

Money works because people and institutions recognize and use it. Different forms of value have different histories, rules, and levels of acceptance; none needs to be treated as sacred. LOVE offers a simple question: if people can place faith in an abstract symbol of value, why not make room for love in that conversation?

Contract design

LOVE has a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000 tokens with 18 decimals, minted once at deployment. The verified contract implements ERC-20 and EIP-2612 permit functionality and has no owner or administrator, additional minting, transfer taxes, blacklist, pause, or upgrade mechanism.
